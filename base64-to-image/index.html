Frequently asked questions

Do I need to include the data:image/…;base64, prefix?

No — the tool accepts both. If your string has a data URL prefix, the image format is read from it; without one, the format is sniffed from the file's magic bytes, defaulting to PNG.

Is my base64 string uploaded anywhere?

Never. Decoding, previewing and conversion all happen with your browser's built-in APIs (atob, Blob, Canvas), so your data never leaves this page.

What happens to transparency when downloading JPG?

JPG doesn't support transparency, so any transparent areas are flattened onto a white background. Download as PNG instead to keep transparency.
