Frequently asked questions

Do my images get uploaded anywhere?

No. Compression happens entirely in your browser — your images never leave your device, and nothing is stored.

How does the target file size mode work?

Enter a size in KB and the tool automatically adjusts the quality (shrinking dimensions if needed) until each image lands close to that size.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP and more can be compressed, and you can output JPG, PNG or WebP — or keep each image's original format. Note that PNG output can sometimes be larger than a photo-style JPG.
