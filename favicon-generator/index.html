      Why include so many sizes?
      
Browsers pick .ico entries automatically, iOS home-screen icons need 180 px, and Android/PWA installs use 192 and 512 px. Covering all four means your icon looks sharp everywhere.

Yes — a genuine ICO container with PNG-compressed 16, 32 and 48 px entries, built with correct headers in your browser.
