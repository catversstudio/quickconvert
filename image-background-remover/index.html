Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model is downloaded to your browser on first use, and the background removal itself runs entirely on your device — your image never leaves this page.

Why does the first run take longer?

First use downloads the AI model (~40MB), which is then cached in your browser — later runs start instantly. You only need an internet connection for that one-time download.

What output do I get?

A PNG file with a transparent background, at the same dimensions as your original image — perfect for thumbnails, product photos and designs.
