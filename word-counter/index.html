Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything is counted locally in your browser with JavaScript — your text never leaves your device.
Is there a character limit?
No hard limit. It handles long documents easily, though very large pastes may feel slower on older phones.
How is reading time calculated?
Using an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute.
