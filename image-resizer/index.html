Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Resizing happens entirely in your browser — your images never leave your device, and nothing is stored.

Will my image look stretched?

Never. The aspect ratio is always locked — when you set a width or a percentage, the height adjusts automatically.

Does resizing change the image format?

No, each image keeps its original format: JPG stays JPG, PNG stays PNG, WebP stays WebP.
