Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Your total word count is divided by your reading speed in words per minute (WPM). For example, a 600-word article at 200 WPM takes 3 minutes to read.

What is a good reading speed in WPM?

The average adult reads at 200–250 WPM, while casual reading can be 150 WPM and fast readers reach 300–400 WPM. Speaking is slower — about 120–150 WPM — which is why the default speaking speed here is 130 WPM.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. All calculations happen locally in your browser with JavaScript. Your text never leaves this page — nothing is uploaded or stored.
