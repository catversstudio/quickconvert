Frequently asked questions

What duration format should I use?

Use hours and minutes like 2:30 or 02:30 — anything up to 999:59 is fine. You can also type just minutes, e.g. 90 becomes 1h 30m.

Does the "Time Between" mode handle times past midnight?

Yes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, it is treated as the next day — for example 22:00 to 06:00 gives 8 hours.

Can the total go negative?

Yes — if you subtract more than you add, the total shows as negative (e.g. −1h 30m) so you can see exactly where you stand.
