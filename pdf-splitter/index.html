    Frequently asked questions

    Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

    No. The PDF is processed with the pdf-lib library running inside your browser, so your document never leaves this page — nothing is uploaded or stored.

    What does a page range like "1-3, 5, 8-10" mean?

    Pages are numbered from 1. Commas separate items, and a dash means "through" — so 1-3, 5, 8-10 selects pages 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 9 and 10, saved into one new PDF in order.

    Can it split password-protected PDFs?

    Encrypted PDFs can't be processed unless you remove the password first, as the browser cannot unlock them. Large files also need enough device memory to load.
