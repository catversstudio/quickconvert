Frequently asked questions

Why does sin(30) give 0.5? Is that degrees or radians?

The calculator defaults to Degrees mode (DEG), like a school calculator — so sin(30) = 0.5. Switch to RAD mode above the keypad if you need radian angles.

Is my calculation data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is computed locally in your browser with the math.js library — no servers, no sign-up, no tracking of your expressions.

What does "Error" mean?

It means the expression couldn't be evaluated — for example an unmatched parenthesis, a missing operand like "5 +", or a mathematically undefined result like 0/0. Press C and try again.
