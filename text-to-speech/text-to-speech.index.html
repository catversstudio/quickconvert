Frequently asked questions

Is my text sent to a server?

No. This tool uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis, so your text never leaves your device — no uploads, no network, no tracking.

Which voices can I choose from?

The voices come from your operating system and browser. Chrome, Edge and Safari ship with a good selection; the exact list varies by device.

Can I download the speech as an audio file?

Not from this tool — it plays speech live through your speakers. If you need a recording, use your device's built-in voice recorder while it plays.
