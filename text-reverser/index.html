Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. All reversing happens with plain JavaScript in your browser — your text never leaves this page and nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.

How are emojis and special characters handled?

The reverser is Unicode-aware: it flips text by code point, so emojis and characters like Hindi or Odia script reverse correctly instead of breaking into fragments.

What is the difference between the three modes?

Reverse whole text flips every character of the entire input. Reverse each line flips the characters of every line separately, keeping line order. Reverse word order keeps each word intact but flips their order within each line.
