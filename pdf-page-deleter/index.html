Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is opened with pdf.js and rebuilt with pdf-lib entirely inside your browser, so the file never leaves your device.

What happens to my original file?

Nothing — it is never modified. The tool creates a brand-new PDF containing only the pages you kept, which you then download.

Can I delete every page?

No. Deleting all pages would leave an empty file, so the tool blocks it and asks you to leave at least one page unselected.
