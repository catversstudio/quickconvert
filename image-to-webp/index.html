Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens with your browser's built-in canvas API, so your images never leave this page — nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.

Which formats can I convert, and is there a limit?

JPG/JPEG and PNG inputs are supported, and you can convert as many images as you want in one batch. Each converted file keeps its original dimensions and is saved as a .webp file.

Will WebP look worse than my original?

At quality 80, WebP files are typically 25–35% smaller than JPEG/PNG with virtually no visible difference. Lower quality shrinks files further; raise it toward 100 for maximum fidelity.
