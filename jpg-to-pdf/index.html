Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is built on this page with the pdf-lib library running in your browser — your images never leave your device. The only network request is loading the library script itself from a CDN.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP. JPEGs and PNGs are embedded directly; WebP images are converted losslessly in your browser first, then embedded.

What is the difference between "Fit to image" and "A4"?

Fit to image makes each PDF page exactly the size of that image, so nothing is cropped or stretched. A4 puts every image centred on a standard A4 page (210 × 297 mm), scaled to fit — handy for printing.
