Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. EXIF metadata is read entirely inside your browser with JavaScript — your photo never leaves this page, is never uploaded and is never stored.

Why does my image show "No metadata found"?

PNGs, screenshots, memes and images re-saved or edited by many apps contain no EXIF data, or have it stripped. JPEG and TIFF photos taken directly by a camera or phone almost always include it.

Why should I be careful with GPS metadata?

Photos with location data reveal the exact spot where they were taken. Social networks usually strip this automatically, but files shared directly (e.g. via messaging or email) keep it — check the GPS row above before sharing.
