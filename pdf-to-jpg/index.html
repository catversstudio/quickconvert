Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is processed entirely inside your browser — it never leaves your device, and nothing is stored anywhere.

What quality will the images be?

You choose the quality: 150, 200 or 300 DPI. JPG pages are saved at high quality and PNG pages are fully lossless, so text stays crisp.

Is there a page limit or a watermark?

No watermark, ever. There is no hard page limit — very large PDFs (50+ pages) just take a little longer to convert.
