Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser using PDF.js, so your PDF never leaves this page — nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.

What DPI should I choose?

150 DPI is great for quick previews and sharing, 200 DPI is the balanced default, and 300 DPI gives print-quality PNGs (larger files).

Can I convert password-protected or very large PDFs?

Password-protected PDFs are not supported — the browser cannot open them without the password. Very large PDFs (many pages at 300 DPI) may use a lot of memory; try 150 DPI if your device struggles.
