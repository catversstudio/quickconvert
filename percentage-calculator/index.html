Frequently asked questions

Are my numbers private?

Yes. Every calculation runs in your browser with JavaScript — nothing you type is sent to any server or stored anywhere.

What does the % change mode show?

How much a value grew or shrank between two numbers — for example 100 → 150 is a +50% increase, 100 → 80 is a −20% decrease. The formula line shows an arrow and the word "increase" or "decrease".

Why does it say "undefined" for some inputs?

Division by zero has no answer, so "X is what % of Y" with Y = 0 and "% change" with an old value of 0 show a warning instead of a number.
