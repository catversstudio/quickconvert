Frequently asked questions

What is a URL slug?

A slug is the readable part of a URL that identifies a page, e.g. how-to-bake-cake in yoursite.com/blog/how-to-bake-cake. Short, lowercase, hyphen-separated slugs are easier to read, share and rank in search results.

Should my slugs be lowercase with hyphens?

Yes — lowercase slugs with hyphens are the widely recommended convention. Hyphens are treated as word separators by search engines, while underscores are not. This tool defaults to exactly that setup.

What does removing stop words do?

Stop words are tiny filler words like "a", "an", "the", "and" or "or". Dropping them makes slugs shorter and punchier — "the-best-cake-recipe" becomes "best-cake-recipe" — without losing meaning.
