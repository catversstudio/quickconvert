Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser with the canvas API — your image never leaves this page, nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.

Does the download keep the original quality and size?

Yes. The download renders at your image's full original resolution. PNG keeps transparency; JPG is exported at 92% quality with a white background (JPEG cannot store transparency).

Do rotations and flips combine?

Yes. Each button applies on top of the current result — for example, Rotate Right twice equals Rotate 180°, and you can then flip it. Use Reset to go back to the original.
