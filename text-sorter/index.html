Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser with JavaScript — your lines never leave this page and nothing is stored anywhere.

What does "Numeric sort" do?

It sorts embedded numbers the way a human would: "Item 2" comes before "Item 10" instead of after it. Useful for filenames, chapter lists and numbered items.

How are duplicates and empty lines removed?

"Remove duplicates" keeps only the first occurrence of each exact duplicate line. "Remove empty lines" drops blank or whitespace-only lines. Use both together for a clean, unique list.
