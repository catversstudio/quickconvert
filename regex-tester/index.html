Frequently asked questions

Is my test text uploaded anywhere?

No. The pattern is compiled and tested with your browser's built-in JavaScript RegExp engine, so everything stays on this page — nothing is uploaded or stored.

What do the g, i, m, s and u flags do?

g (global) finds all matches instead of just the first; i makes matching case-insensitive; m lets ^ and $ match at line boundaries; s lets the dot match newlines too; u enables full Unicode mode.

What does the match list show?

Every match with its character index in the test string, plus each captured group from parentheses — so you can see exactly what your groups captured.
