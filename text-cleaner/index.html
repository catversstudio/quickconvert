Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. All cleaning happens with plain JavaScript inside your browser, so your text never leaves this page — nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.

What counts as an empty line?

Any line that contains no text, or only spaces and tabs. When "Remove empty lines" is on, all of them are deleted.

Does "Remove line breaks" join words together?

No. Lines are joined with a single space between them, so words never merge into one. If "Remove extra spaces" is also on, any double spaces from the join are collapsed.
