Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Your image is drawn onto a canvas right here in your browser, so it never leaves your device. There is no upload, no account and nothing stored anywhere.

What resolution is the download?

Full resolution — the export is rendered on a canvas at your image's original pixel dimensions, so the watermarked file is exactly as sharp as the file you uploaded.

Which formats can I upload and download?

You can upload JPG, PNG, WebP and any other image format your browser can open. Downloads are exported as PNG (keeps transparency) or JPG (smaller file size, with a white background).
