Frequently asked questions

Is my HTML uploaded to a server?

No. Formatting and minifying run entirely in your browser using the js-beautify library, so your code never leaves this page — nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.

What does Format do exactly?

It pretty-prints your HTML with consistent indentation (2 or 4 spaces, your choice), one element per line, and collapses stray blank lines — works on full documents and on fragments or snippets too.

What does Minify do?

It removes comments and collapses unnecessary whitespace to shrink the file for production. Content inside <pre>, <textarea>, <script> and <style> tags is left untouched so your code keeps working.
