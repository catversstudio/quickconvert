Frequently asked questions

Is my resume data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser on this page — nothing is uploaded, sent or stored on any server.

How do I save my resume as a PDF?

Press “Print / Save as PDF”, then choose “Save as PDF” as the printer destination in the print dialog. Only the resume itself is printed — the website's header, form and menus are hidden automatically.

Can I add more than one job or degree?

Yes. Use the “+ Add experience” and “+ Add education” buttons to add as many entries as you need, and the Remove button on any entry to delete it.
