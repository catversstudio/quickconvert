Frequently asked questions

How is the average calculated?

It is the arithmetic mean: all valid numbers are added together and the total is divided by how many valid numbers there are. For example, the average of 12, 45 and 78 is (12 + 45 + 78) ÷ 3 = 45.

What happens to entries that are not numbers?

Anything that is not a valid number (like "abc" or "12x") is ignored in the calculation and listed under "Ignored entries" so you can spot typos in your pasted data.

Can I mix commas, spaces and line breaks?

Yes — commas, spaces, semicolons, tabs and newlines all work as separators, and you can mix them freely. Empty gaps are skipped automatically.
