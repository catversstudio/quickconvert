Frequently asked questions

How are the colors chosen?

The image is downscaled and thousands of its pixels are grouped by median-cut quantization, which repeatedly splits the color space along the widest channel. Each swatch is the average of its group, ranked by how much of the image it covers.

Why might two swatches look similar?

Median-cut splits purely by statistics, so two groups can land near each other on photos with smooth gradients. Exact duplicates are removed automatically.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. Sampling and quantization run entirely in your browser with plain JavaScript — no libraries, no servers.
