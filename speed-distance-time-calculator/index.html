Frequently asked questions

What formulas does this tool use?

The classic trio: speed = distance ÷ time, distance = speed × time, and time = distance ÷ speed. Every calculation is done in SI units internally and then converted to your chosen output unit.

How do I convert 50 km/h to m/s?

Divide by 3.6: 50 km/h = 13.89 m/s. Solve-for-speed mode shows your answer in km/h, m/s and mph automatically. Conversely, multiply m/s by 3.6 to get km/h.

Can I mix miles with hours, or metres with seconds?

Yes — every input and the output has its own unit selector. 1 mile = 1609.344 metres exactly, and the tool converts consistently before applying the formula.
