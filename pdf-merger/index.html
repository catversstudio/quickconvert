Frequently asked questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. Merging happens entirely inside your browser using a client-side PDF library. Your files never leave your device — not even temporarily.

How do I change the order of the merged pages?

After adding your PDFs, use the up and down arrow buttons on each file row. The files are merged in exactly the order shown in your list.

Is there a limit on the number or size of PDFs?

There's no hard limit — but very large files are processed in your browser's memory, so merging dozens of huge PDFs may be slower or run into your device's memory. For typical documents it just works.
