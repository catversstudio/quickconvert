Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Cropping happens entirely in your browser with the canvas API — your image never leaves this page and is never stored anywhere.

What quality is the downloaded crop?

The crop is cut from your original image at full resolution. PNG downloads are lossless; JPG downloads use high quality (92%).

Can I change the aspect ratio after drawing the box?

Yes — tap any ratio pill and the box reshapes around its center while keeping the current center point. "Free" lets you draw any shape.
