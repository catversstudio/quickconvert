Frequently asked questions

How does the pixelation work?

The image is shrunk to a tiny grid and scaled back up with smoothing turned off, so each block becomes one flat color — the classic pixel-art effect.

Is the downloaded image full resolution?

Yes. The preview is downscaled for speed, but the download re-renders the effect at your photo's original resolution.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The effect is drawn on a canvas in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
