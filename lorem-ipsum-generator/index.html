Frequently asked questions

What is lorem ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is placeholder text used in design and publishing to fill space before the final copy is ready. It comes from a scrambled passage of Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" (45 BC), and has been the industry standard dummy text for centuries.

Is the generated text random?

Yes — every generation is fresh. Sentences and paragraphs are assembled randomly from the classic lorem ipsum word bank. Turn on the toggle to start the first paragraph with the traditional "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet" opening line.

Is my generated text uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is generated locally in your browser with plain JavaScript — nothing is sent to a server, uploaded, or stored anywhere.
