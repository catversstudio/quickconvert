Frequently asked questions

Is the downloaded image full resolution?

Yes. The preview is downscaled for speed, but the download re-renders the blur at your photo's original resolution.

How does region blur work?

You draw a rectangle on the preview; only that area is blended with a blurred copy while the rest stays sharp. Perfect for hiding faces, plates or text.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Blurring runs entirely on a canvas in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
