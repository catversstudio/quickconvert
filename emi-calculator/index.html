Frequently asked questions

How is the EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of monthly instalments. It uses the standard reducing-balance method, so interest is charged only on the outstanding principal.

Does this assume a fixed interest rate?

Yes. The calculator assumes a fixed rate for the entire tenure with monthly compounding. Floating-rate loans may differ from the estimate if the lender changes the rate later.

Should I choose a shorter or longer tenure?

A shorter tenure means a higher monthly EMI but much less total interest paid; a longer tenure lowers the EMI but increases the total interest. Use the split bar above to see the trade-off for your own numbers before deciding.
