Frequently asked questions

How are words counted?

A word is any run of characters separated by whitespace (spaces, tabs or new lines). Multiple spaces in a row don't create empty words, so your count matches what you'd expect.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes an average reading speed of 200 words per minute. It's a rough estimate — dense or technical text may take longer.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Counting happens entirely in your browser with plain JavaScript — your text never leaves this page and nothing is stored anywhere.
