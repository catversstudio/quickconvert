Frequently asked questions

    

      What are meta tags and why do they matter?
      
Meta tags describe your page to search engines and social platforms. A good title and description improve your search-result appearance, while Open Graph and Twitter card tags control the preview image and text when your link is shared on social media.

    

    

      Where do I paste the generated snippet?
      
Inside the <head>…</head> section of your HTML page, near the top. The snippet is a complete, ready-to-paste block.

    

    

      Is any of my input sent to a server?
      
No. Everything is generated with JavaScript running locally in your browser — your site details never leave this page.
