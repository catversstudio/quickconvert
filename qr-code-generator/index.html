Frequently asked questions

Do the QR codes expire?
No. The code is generated on your device and encodes your text directly — it works forever, with no account or expiry.
Is my data private?
Yes. QR generation happens 100% in your browser; nothing you type is sent to any server.
How much text can a QR code hold?
Up to a few thousand characters depending on the error-correction level. If your text is too long, the tool will tell you to shorten it.
