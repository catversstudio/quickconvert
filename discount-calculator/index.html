Frequently asked questions

How are the discount and the extra coupon combined?

Multiplicatively, exactly like real stores do — the second discount applies to the already-discounted price, not the original. So 20% off plus an extra 10% coupon is 28% total off (1 − 0.80 × 0.90 = 0.28), not 30%.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. Every calculation happens with plain JavaScript in your browser — your prices never leave this page, and nothing is stored anywhere.

Which currency does the calculator use?

₹ by default. The tool is currency-neutral — the maths is identical for any currency, so just read ₹ as your own currency symbol.
