Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum attendance required?

Most colleges and universities in India require at least 75% attendance to be eligible for examinations, so this calculator uses 75% as the threshold for the Safe zone.

How do the Safe, At risk and Critical statuses work?

Safe means your attendance is 75% or higher. At risk covers 60–74% — you need to attend several classes in a row to recover. Critical is below 60% — you should attend every class from now on to climb back above 75%.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculation happens entirely in your browser with plain JavaScript — nothing is uploaded, stored or tracked. The page also works fully offline once loaded.
